frameline

A small timeline for marking time. Frame-exact, SMPTE timecode (drop-frame included), keys on rows, and a clock that stays yours.

00:00:00:00

Press the ruler to move the playhead (arrows: ±1 frame, Shift ±1 s). Drag keys — they snap to other keys and the playhead; hold Alt to turn snapping off. Drag on empty space to select, Shift to add. Double-click a row to add a key, Delete to remove. Ctrl/⌘ + wheel zooms. The lower timeline is kept in step with the upper one through the host's clock — nothing echoes.
